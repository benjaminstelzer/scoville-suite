While `.scoville-workflow/guard.json` exists, all tasks read it before project writes. Only its exact coordinator edits Plans, stages, or commits. One activated executor or repair edits only its assigned unit and never delegates. Reviewers, audits, consultations, undefined workers, and other tasks are read-only while the guard exists. A Scoville task is read-only when its required guard is missing or unreadable, or its own required write authorization is pending or mismatched, except for an identity-checked guard transition through the bundled helper. Change guards only through that helper. Writes mean create, edit, delete, rename, format, stage, or commit. Nested instructions may only tighten this contract.
